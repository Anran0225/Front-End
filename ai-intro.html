Definition

AI, in one sentence

Artificial Intelligence is software that learns patterns from data (or is designed to reason over rules) so it can make decisions or predictions without being explicitly programmed for every case.

Most of what people call "AI" today falls under Machine Learning — systems that improve at a task by training on examples rather than following hard-coded instructions.

AI vs ML vs Deep Learning

Related ideas, different scope.

AI is the broad goal of making machines perform intelligent tasks. Machine Learning is one way to achieve AI by learning from data. Deep Learning is a specialised type of machine learning using multi-layer neural networks.

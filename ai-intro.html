What Artificial Intelligence actually is.

A plain-language primer for members starting from zero — no jargon left unexplained.

Definition

AI, in one sentence

Artificial Intelligence is software that learns patterns from data (or is designed to reason over rules) so it can make decisions or predictions without being explicitly programmed for every case.

Most of what people call "AI" today falls under Machine Learning — systems that improve at a task by training on examples rather than following hard-coded instructions.

Three ways machines "learn"

Supervised

Learning from labels

The model sees inputs paired with correct answers (e.g. photos labelled "cat"/"dog") and learns to map one to the other.

Unsupervised

Finding structure

No labels given — the model groups or compresses data based on patterns it discovers on its own.

Reinforcement

Learning by reward

An agent takes actions in an environment and learns which actions earn the highest long-term reward.

Why it matters

AI in daily student life

From spam filters to translation apps to the recommendation engine on your streaming app — AI already runs quietly in the background of most software you use. Understanding it is quickly becoming as fundamental as understanding the internet itself.

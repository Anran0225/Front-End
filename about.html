Origin Log

A club built by builders.

NEXUS started as three friends debugging a neural network at 2am. Today we're a full community of students who believe the best way to learn AI is to ship something with it.

Mission

Make AI hands-on, not theoretical.

Lectures explain how a model works. NEXUS is where you actually train one, break it, and fix it again. We exist to close the gap between "I read a paper on this" and "I built this."

We're organised around three commitments: open access (no prior ML experience required), project-first learning (every member ships something), and community over competition (we build each other up).

How it works

From curious to capable, in four stages

Orientation

Attend your first meetup, meet a mentor, and pick a track — Learn, Build, Practice, or Compete.

Onboarding sprint

A two-week crash course covering Python for ML, prompt basics, and our internal tools.

Join a project pod

Teams of 3–5 members ship a real ML or AI-integrated product over a 6-week cycle.

Demo & showcase

Present at our termly Demo Day, open to the whole faculty and industry guests.
